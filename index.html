Handwriting Standards

Mandatory Penmanship Guidelines

All submitted handwriting sheets must satisfy essential legibility criteria.

Accepted Penmanship Criteria
  • Neat & Legible Script: Uniform spacing between letters, words, and lines (Cursive or Clean Print).
  • Approved Pen Ink: Dark blue or black gel or ballpoint pens as requested per project.
  • Standard Paper Sheets: Clean A4 sheets, ruled college notebooks, or specified project templates.
  • Clear Camera Scans: Well-lit, uncropped, non-blurry photos or PDF scans of handwritten pages.
Unacceptable Handwriting Errors
  • Heavy Overwriting: Excessive white-out fluid, scratched-out words, or visible ink smudges.
  • Illegible Handwriting: Sloppy script, uneven line alignment, or inconsistent font sizes.
  • Faded Ink / Pencil: Submissions completed in faint pencil or leaking ink pens.
  • Incomplete Assignments: Missing pages or skipped paragraphs during paper copying.
